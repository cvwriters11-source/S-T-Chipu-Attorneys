S T Chipu Attorneys

S T Chipu Attorneys is a law firm in Arcadia, Pretoria, advising on medical negligence, Road Accident Fund claims, family and divorce, real estate, criminal matters, administrative law, unlawful arrest and detention, labour disputes, and civil litigation.

The practice serves clients in Pretoria and Gauteng and, where the instruction requires it, further afield. Matters are heard in the magistrates’ courts, the Gauteng Division of the High Court in Pretoria, the CCMA, and the other forums the case calls for.

The firm is a small practice, so a client deals with the attorney handling the file. Fees are explained before an instruction is accepted. The arrangement may be an hourly rate, a fixed amount for a defined task, or a contingency fee where the Contingency Fees Act allows it.

The way the firm works:

  • A weak claim is identified before fees are spent on it.
  • Deadlines are dealt with first. RAF claims, notices to the state, and CCMA referrals cannot be left.
  • Advice is given in ordinary language.
  • The work is defined before the firm acts, and it is discussed again if the matter grows.

An enquiry on this website is not legal advice. It does not appoint the firm, and it does not stop a deadline from running.
