About Us

The practice

S T Chipu Attorneys is a Pretoria law practice. Instructions are taken at Office N0306, MBA Building, 527 Stanza Bopape Street, Arcadia, and the work follows the court or tribunal the matter requires.

People instruct the firm when treatment at a hospital has caused harm, when a road accident has interrupted work and family life, when a marriage is ending, when a property transfer or a dispute over occupation needs care, and when liberty, a job, or a government decision is at stake.

The first task is to say what the law allows. Some claims are strong. Some are late. Some depend on a document the client does not yet have. A consultation should leave you knowing which of those it is, what the next step will cost, and what the firm will not do.

What to bring

Bring what you have. Do not delay a first call because a file is incomplete, especially where a statutory notice or a court date is close.

  • Your identity document, and a short note of the dates that matter.
  • Any court date, charge sheet, summons, or dismissal letter.
  • Hospital name and records, an accident report, or a CAS or CR number.
  • A marriage certificate, offer to purchase, title deed, or lease, if that is the matter.

Where the work is done

Magistrates’ courts, criminal courts, and the Gauteng Division of the High Court in Pretoria. Counsel is briefed where the forum and the matter call for it. Road Accident Fund claims and civil claims against the police follow the notices those statutes require. Labour disputes start at the CCMA or a bargaining council.

What you tell the firm in order to take advice is treated as confidential. The duty to carry the matter begins when the firm accepts the instruction.
