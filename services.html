Services

Medical negligence

Claims for injury caused by treatment in a public hospital or private practice, including delayed diagnosis, surgical injury, and birth-related harm. Claims against the state usually need written notice before summons.

Road Accident Fund

Compensation after a motor accident, including medical costs, loss of income, and loss of support. Identified claims generally prescribe in three years. Unidentified claims usually must be lodged within two years.

Family and divorce

Divorce, care and contact, maintenance, protection orders, and antenuptial contracts, including questions arising from customary marriages. Where children are involved, their best interests govern.

Real estate

Transfers, bond registration, deeds of sale, leases, and eviction. A sale of land must be in writing. Eviction from a home is regulated, and a court considers the people living there.

Criminal law

Representation from arrest and bail through plea, trial, and, where the record supports it, appeal or review. You have the right to remain silent and to consult an attorney.

Administrative law

Reviews of decisions by departments, municipalities, and other public bodies. A PAJA review is generally brought within 180 days of knowing the decision and the reasons.

Unlawful arrest and detention

Civil claims against the Minister of Police for arrest or detention without lawful grounds. Written notice to the state is generally required within six months.

Labour law

Dismissal, discipline, discrimination, and CCMA disputes, for employees and employers. An unfair dismissal referral is usually 30 days, and an unfair labour practice usually 90 days.

Litigation and disputes

Civil claims, letters of demand, interdicts, debt recovery, and settlement in the magistrates’ courts and the High Court. If a summons has been served, bring it at once.
